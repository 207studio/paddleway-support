This policy describes Paddleway for Mac and iPad, provided by Yooil Jang. It also explains the outside services the app uses. Paddleway does not operate a server that stores your remote desktop content.

1. Screen content and input

Screen frames, pointer and keyboard events, and dictated text travel over an encrypted connection between your devices. The Mac app needs Screen Recording permission to capture the selected display and Accessibility permission to deliver your input. Paddleway does not upload this content to a developer-operated storage or analytics service. The receiving app may hold data in memory while displaying a session.

2. Tailscale connectivity

Paddleway embeds Tailscale networking. Tailscale and the sign-in provider you choose process information needed to authenticate and connect your devices. This can include your account name and email, account/device identifiers, device and host names, public keys, operating system and client version, IP addresses, and connection or diagnostic metadata. Tailscale processes this information under its own retention and privacy practices. Network relays carry encrypted traffic; this is separate from connection metadata.

See Tailscale’s privacy policy and security information. Paddleway’s developer does not receive your Tailscale password. Signing in does not share your tailnet with the developer.

3. Dictation

The microphone and speech-recognition permissions are requested when you use dictation. Paddleway uses Apple’s Speech framework and prefers on-device recognition when available. Otherwise, audio may be sent to Apple for recognition. Apple’s handling of audio and transcripts is governed by its services and your settings. Paddleway does not record audio files or send audio to OpenAI. Recognized text is delivered to the active input target on your Mac.

Read Apple’s Siri, Dictation & Privacy information. You can stop dictation with the microphone button and revoke microphone or speech access in iPad Settings.

4. Music

With your permission, the music panel accesses media-library information and the Apple Music system player, including titles, artists, artwork and playback state. Paddleway does not upload your library to its developer. Apple Music’s own account, purchase and privacy conditions apply. You can revoke media-library access in iPad Settings.

5. Data stored on your devices

Paddleway keeps connection credentials, Tailscale state, the selected host and preferences such as frame rate, sensitivity and panel arrangement on your device. Connection secrets use platform-provided secure storage where implemented; Tailscale also maintains local network state. Local diagnostic/status files may include connection state, errors and frame statistics. These files are not automatically sent to the developer. Local files remain until removed, and keychain items can outlast deleting an app.

6. Choices, deletion and retention

Quit the apps to end use, revoke Mac/iPad permissions in System Settings or Settings, and remove Paddleway devices from your Tailscale administration page to revoke their network membership. Removing the app alone does not delete your Tailscale account or all keychain credentials. Tailscale account deletion and retention requests go to Tailscale; Apple service data requests go to Apple. Contact Paddleway support for help removing remaining local app state. We do not keep a developer-side archive of your sessions to delete.

If you voluntarily submit a support request, the information you provide is used to respond and investigate that request. The support tracker is hosted by GitHub, and public issues are visible to others. Its storage and account controls are governed by GitHub’s privacy statement. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process request and access information. This site has no added advertising or analytics scripts.

7. Advertising and tracking

Paddleway contains no advertising SDK and does not sell remote desktop content or use it for advertising. It does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising. This does not mean outside connectivity and Apple services collect no data; those services are described above.

8. Contact and changes

Provider: Yooil Jang. Use Paddleway support for privacy questions and requests. Please do not include passwords, connection keys or private screen content in a public issue. Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

한국어 안내

패들웨이는 Yooil Jang이 제공하는 Mac·iPad 원격 데스크톱 앱입니다. 화면과 입력, 전사한 문장은 암호화하여 본인의 기기로 전송하며, 개발자 서버에 세션 내용을 저장하지 않습니다.

Tailscale은 로그인과 연결을 위해 계정·기기 식별 정보, 이름·이메일, IP 주소, 기기·운영체제 정보와 연결·진단 정보를 처리할 수 있습니다. 해당 정보의 보관과 삭제는 Tailscale 정책을 따릅니다. 개발자는 사용자의 Tailscale 암호를 받거나 사용자의 네트워크에 자동으로 참여하지 않습니다.

받아쓰기는 Apple의 음성 인식을 사용합니다. 가능하면 기기에서 처리하지만, 지원되지 않으면 음성이 Apple로 전송될 수 있습니다. 패들웨이는 음성 파일을 녹음해 보관하거나 OpenAI로 전송하지 않습니다. 음악 정보는 허용한 보관함과 Apple Music 시스템 플레이어에서 가져오며 개발자에게 업로드하지 않습니다.

연결 자격 정보, Tailscale 상태, 선택한 Mac과 조작 설정, 진단 상태는 기기에 저장됩니다. 앱을 삭제해도 키체인 항목이나 Tailscale 계정은 남을 수 있습니다. 설정에서 권한을 취소하고, Tailscale 관리 페이지에서 해당 기기를 제거할 수 있습니다. 남은 로컬 데이터 삭제는 지원 페이지로 문의해 주세요.

광고 SDK나 광고용 앱 간 추적을 사용하지 않습니다. 문의 내용은 문의 처리 목적으로 사용되며, 공개 GitHub 문의에는 민감한 정보를 올리지 마세요. 웹사이트와 문의 시스템의 운영에는 GitHub 개인정보 정책이 적용됩니다. 개인정보 문의는 지원 페이지에서 접수합니다.
